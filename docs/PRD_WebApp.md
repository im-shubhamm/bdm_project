# PRD — Premier League Transfer Value Intelligence (BDM)

## Problem

Premier League clubs spend hundreds of millions each window. Owners need a clear answer: **did this fee buy on-pitch output?**

## Goal

Ship an interactive dashboard that scores every arrival-season transfer on Impact Points per £m and rolls the score up to clubs, roles, nationalities, fee brackets and seasons — same analytical pattern as auction-value BDM projects in cricket, applied to football.

## Users

- BDM faculty and classmates during a live demo
- Framing: a club analytics desk advising a sporting director before the next window

## Pages

1. Home — KPIs, explore prompt, insight carousel  
2. Market — spend and average fee by season  
3. Clubs — Value Index, spend vs win %, matrix  
4. Value Map — fee vs impact scatter with quadrants  
5. Steals & Overpaid — rankings + failed targets  
6. Roles — role × English/Overseas  
7. Brackets — fee bands  
8. Players — search + profile  
9. Squad Builder — £m budget simulator  
10. Methodology — formulas and team credits  

## Non-goals

- Live Opta / StatsBomb feed
- Full Financial Fair Play legal model
- Native Power BI `.pbix` binary (web app is the graded interactive surface)
