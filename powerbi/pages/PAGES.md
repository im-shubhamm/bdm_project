# Suggested Power BI pages (for presentation)

## 1. Overview
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Orders, Loss Lines
- Line chart: Sales & Profit by Year / Month
- Donut: Sales by Category
- Slicers: Year, Region, Segment

## 2. Category deep-dive
- Bar: Sales vs Profit by Category
- Horizontal bar: Profit by SubCategory (conditional format red if &lt; 0)
- Matrix: Category × Region with Profit Margin %
- **So what text box:** Tables & Bookcases destroy Furniture profit

## 3. Discount impact
- Clustered column: Sales & Profit by DiscountBand
- Scatter: Discount (X) vs Profit (Y), size = Sales
- Table: worst products by Profit

## 4. Customers (RFM)
- Cards: Champions / At Risk counts
- Bar: Sales by RFMSegment
- Table: top 20 customers

## 5. Methodology
- Source URL, row counts, star-schema diagram (see docs/report)
- Team names + roll numbers
